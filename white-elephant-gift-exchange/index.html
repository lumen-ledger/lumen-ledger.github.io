White Elephant gift exchange: turn order and steal tracker

Paste the players, draw a random turn order, then use the tracker during the game: it counts how many times each gift has been stolen and shows when a gift is locked. Works for White Elephant, Yankee Swap and Dirty Santa. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is saved or sent.

Standard rules in 6 steps

  1. Everyone brings one wrapped gift within an agreed budget and puts it in a central pile. Gifts are anonymous.
  2. Draw the turn order (numbers from a hat, or the tool above).
  3. Player 1 opens a gift from the pile. The turn ends.
  4. Every next player chooses: open a new gift from the pile, or steal any gift already opened.
  5. A player who is stolen from either opens a new gift or steals a different one. They cannot steal straight back the gift just taken from them. The chain continues until someone opens a new gift; then the next number plays.
  6. The game ends when the last gift is opened. With the first-player rule, player 1 (who had no choice at the start) may then swap once with anyone whose gift is not locked.

There is no official rulebook: these are the common house rules. Agree on the budget, the steal limit and the first-player rule before you start, and say them out loud, because most disputes come from rules nobody announced.

Budget and timing

  • Budget: pick one round number and say it in the invite (for example $20 or $25). A cap with no minimum leads to uneven gifts; "about $25" works better than "up to $25". To plan all your holiday gifts, see the holiday budget planner. Swapping cookies instead of gifts? The cookie exchange calculator works out how many to bake, and the Cookie Exchange Planner spreadsheet (pay what you want, from $0) adds a sign-up sheet and allergen labels.
  • Timing: as a rough planning figure, allow 1 to 2 minutes per player plus extra for steal chains; a steal limit keeps the total predictable. This is a planning assumption, not a measured average.
  • Odd numbers, late arrivals: a latecomer can take the next free number at the end of the order; there is no need to redraw.

FAQ

Is White Elephant the same as Secret Santa?

No. In Secret Santa each person buys for one specific person drawn in advance. In White Elephant gifts are anonymous and players open or steal them in turn. For a classic draw with private links, use the Secret Santa generator.

How many times can a gift be stolen?

It depends on your house rules. Many groups lock a gift after 2 or 3 steals; set your number in the tool above. With 0 there is no limit.

Can I steal back a gift that was just taken from me?

Under the usual rules, not immediately: you must open a new gift or steal a different one. You may steal it back on a later turn if it is not locked.

What does player 1 get at the end?

Player 1 opened a gift with nothing to compare. To balance that, many groups let player 1 swap once with any unlocked gift after the last gift is opened. Untick the box if your group does not play that way.

Is the turn order really random?

Yes. The tool shuffles the names with the browser's cryptographic random number generator (Fisher-Yates shuffle). Redraw gives a new order; nothing is stored.

How many players do I need?

At least 3; the game gets more interesting from about 6. In large groups (20 or more) use a steal limit or the one-steal-per-turn variant to keep it under an hour.

Does it work for a virtual party?

Yes: share your screen with the order and the tracker. With remote players, gifts are usually shown on camera or as photos and shipped afterwards by whoever ends up with them.
