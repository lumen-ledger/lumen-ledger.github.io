UK bank holidays 2027: annual leave planner

In 2027 England and Wales have 8 bank holidays, Scotland 9 and Northern Ireland 10. Christmas Day and Boxing Day fall on a weekend, so the substitute days are Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December. With 3 days of leave (29–31 December) you get 10 days off in a row, from Saturday 25 December to Monday 3 January 2028. The planner below finds the best plan for your own number of days.

General information, not advice. When you can take leave depends on your contract and your employer: some employers count bank holidays as part of your annual leave, some ask you to work them, and your leave year may not run from January to December. The planner only knows the bank holidays and the extra days off you enter.

    In Scotland Easter Monday is not a bank holiday, so 4 days of leave around Easter give 9 days off rather than 10 (Sat 20 – Sun 28 March, taking 22–25 March). The planner shows the exact result for your settings.

    How the planner works

    • Days off: Sundays, Saturdays (unless you work Monday–Saturday), the bank holidays of the nation you pick, and any extra days you enter.
    • A break is a run of consecutive days off that contains at least one day of annual leave; its length must be between the shortest and longest break you choose. The shortest break stops the planner from recommending nothing but long weekends.
    • The planner checks every combination of breaks (dynamic programming over the days of the year) and returns the one with the most days off in total; on a tie, the one that uses fewer leave days. It is not a rule of thumb: the result is the optimum for your settings (tested against exhaustive search on 300 random calendars).
    • Breaks must start in 2027; the calendar runs to 6 January 2028, so a Christmas break can end in the new year. Leave taken in January 2028 usually comes out of the next year's allowance.
    • Statutory leave for most workers is 5.6 weeks a year, capped at 28 days (Working Time Regulations 1998, regulations 13 and 13A). For a 5-day week that is 28 days, and an employer may count bank holidays within it: in that case enter only the days left after the bank holidays.

    Sources (checked 13 November 2026)

    • Bank holiday dates for 2027 and January 2028, for each nation: GOV.UK, UK bank holidays (official data feed bank-holidays.json). An extra bank holiday can be added by royal proclamation: if that happens, GOV.UK will list it.
    • Bank holidays and annual leave: GOV.UK, Holiday entitlement: bank holidays ("Bank or public holidays do not have to be given as paid leave"; an employer can include them in statutory leave).
    • Statutory entitlement: Working Time Regulations 1998, regulation 13A (additional 1.6 weeks; total with regulation 13 capped at 28 days). Northern Ireland has its own Working Time Regulations; check nidirect for the rules there.
