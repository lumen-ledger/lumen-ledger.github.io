Secret Santa generator: exclusions, private links, no email

Type the names, keep couples or housemates apart, set the budget. You get one private link per person and send each link yourself, by any chat app. The draw happens in your browser: no account, no email addresses, nothing saved on a server.

Make sure nobody is looking at your screen.

This page read your assignment from the link itself; nothing was downloaded from a server. Keep the link: you can open it again whenever you like. Organize your own draw.

Draw names

Random numbers come from your browser's cryptographic generator (crypto.getRandomValues). The names are not sent anywhere and disappear when you close the tab, so copy the links before closing.

How the private links work

  1. Each link contains that person's own assignment (their name, who they buy for, the limit, the date and your message), stored after the # sign. The part after # is never sent to the web server by browsers, so this site never receives it.
  2. The assignment is encoded, not encrypted: it is not readable at a glance, but someone determined to decode a link could. It stops accidental spoilers, not a determined snoop. Send each person only their own link.
  3. If you take part too, do not open other people's links. Use "Copy all links" to keep a list for re-sending: it shows who each link belongs to, not who they drew.
  4. If someone loses their link, send them the same link again from your list. A new draw gives everyone new assignments, so all the old links would have to be replaced.

Tips for a smooth exchange

  • Exclusions: couples and people who live together usually swap gifts anyway, so keep them apart. If there are too many exclusions the draw can be impossible: the tool tells you, and you can remove one.
  • Limit: a clear spending limit avoids awkward gaps. To check what the whole season costs you, see the gift budget per person calculator.
  • Hosting the meal too? The Christmas Dinner Planner (free spreadsheet, pay what you want) works the oven schedule back from the time you eat. Direct download: christmas-dinner-planner.xlsx.
  • Gift cards: in the US, money on most store gift cards cannot expire for at least 5 years: gift card rules and date calculator.
  • Returns: buying online in the UK? Gift return deadlines (14-day cancellation, 30 days for faulty goods).

FAQ

Do I need everyone's email address? No. You choose how to deliver each link: text message, chat app, email, or written on a card.

Can the organizer see who drew whom? Only if they choose to. After the draw you see the list of links; the assignments stay hidden unless you click "Show full list", meant for an organizer who is not taking part.

Is the draw random? Yes, within your rules: nobody draws themselves or someone excluded, and, if the box is ticked, nobody ends up in a two-person swap. With exclusions some combinations are impossible, which also makes some outcomes more or less likely than in a draw without rules.

Is anything saved? No. The page has no tracking, no cookies and no account. See Privacy.
