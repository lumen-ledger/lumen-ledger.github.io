How this works. Yearly output = panel W × peak sun hours × 365 × mounting factor × (1 − shading) × 0.86 system efficiency (inverter, wiring, heat), capped by the inverter limit. To estimate how much you use directly, an average day is modelled as a bell curve over ~12 daylight hours and compared hour by hour with your baseload: anything above baseload is exported. In the conservative model (default) yearly output is split across months using the monthly profile from NREL PVWatts v8 for a south-facing 30° array near 40°N (Columbus, Ohio; TMY weather, queried 2026-10-30), and in each month half the days produce 1.5× the average and half 0.5×. The chart always shows the average day. Panels lose 0.5% output per year. These are estimates for comparing options, not a guarantee. Check your state law, your utility's rules and your lease before installing.
