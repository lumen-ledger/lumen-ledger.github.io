Turkey planner: when to start thawing, when it goes in the oven

Weight + dinner time → thaw start, oven time at 325 °F, first thermometer check. Example: a 16 lb turkey for a 3:00 pm dinner on Thursday, November 26, 2026 goes in the fridge to thaw by Saturday, November 21 and in the oven by 10:25 AM.

General information, not food-safety advice for your situation. Times are approximate: the turkey is done only when a food thermometer reads 165 °F in the thickest part of the breast, the innermost part of the thigh and the wing. Tables from University of Georgia Extension Circular 1226 (reviewed 2026-09-15), which is based on USDA FSIS guidance, read 2026-10-31. The USDA and FoodSafety.gov pages refused automated reading that day, so check them before cooking: USDA FSIS “Let's Talk Turkey”.

How big a turkey for your guests?

Rule of thumb from University of Illinois Extension: about 1 lb of whole turkey (raw weight, bone in) per person, 1½ lb for hearty eaters or plenty of leftovers, ½ lb per person for a bone-in breast. If you serve another meat as well, the lower amount is usually enough. Above 24 lb the tables below stop, so the tool suggests two smaller turkeys, which also roast faster.

Planner

Built in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

How the plan is built: the turkey comes out of the oven 20 minutes before dinner to rest. It goes in early enough for the longest roasting time in its weight row, and you start checking the temperature at the shortest time. If it reaches 165 °F early, cover it with foil while it waits. Thawing starts early enough for the longest time in the thawing row. When a weight sits on the border of two rows (for example 12 or 16 lb), the planner uses the shortest and longest times across both rows.

  • Refrigerator: the safest method. Keep the turkey in its wrapper on a tray on the bottom shelf. Starting a day early is fine.
  • Cold water: keep the turkey in a leak-proof bag, submerged in cold tap water. Change the water every 30 minutes, and cook it right after it thaws. A 20 lb turkey means 10–12 hours of water changes.
  • From frozen: it is safe, but it takes at least 50% longer. Remove the giblet bag with tongs once the turkey has partly thawed in the oven. The planner multiplies the time by 1.5, which is a minimum.
  • Never thaw a turkey on the counter: its surface stays in the 40–140 °F danger zone, where bacteria multiply fast.
  • Bake the stuffing in a separate dish until it reaches 165 °F in the center.

Microwave thawing: safe only if the bird fits. The minutes per pound and the power level depend on the oven, so take them from your microwave's manual (no single figure applies to every model), then cook the turkey straight away (University of Nebraska–Lincoln Extension, "How to Thaw a Turkey", reviewed 2026, read 2026-11-14).

After dinner

  • Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours (1 hour above 90 °F), in shallow containers.
  • Use turkey, stuffing and gravy within 3–4 days, or freeze them (best within 2–6 months).
  • Reheat leftovers to 165 °F.

Quick answers

From the same tables and sources as the planner (listed below).

When should I take the turkey out of the freezer for Thanksgiving 2026?

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26, 2026. For a 3 PM dinner and fridge thawing at 40 °F, using the slow end of each range: about 12 lb by Sunday, November 22; about 16 lb by Saturday, November 21; 20 to 24 lb by Friday, November 20. Weights on the boundary between two table rows use the longer row.

How long does a turkey take to thaw in the fridge?

In a refrigerator at 40 °F: 1 to 3 days for 4 to 12 lb, 3 to 4 days for 12 to 16 lb, 4 to 5 days for 16 to 20 lb, 5 to 6 days for 20 to 24 lb.

How long does a turkey take to thaw in cold water?

2 to 6 hours for 4 to 12 lb, 6 to 8 hours for 12 to 16 lb, 8 to 10 hours for 16 to 20 lb, 10 to 12 hours for 20 to 24 lb. Change the water every 30 minutes and cook the turkey right after it thaws.

How long do I roast a turkey at 325 °F?

Fresh or thawed, unstuffed: 2 h 45 min to 3 h for 8 to 12 lb, 3 h to 3 h 45 min for 12 to 14 lb, 3 h 45 min to 4 h 15 min for 14 to 18 lb, 4 h 15 min to 4 h 30 min for 18 to 20 lb, 4 h 30 min to 5 h for 20 to 24 lb. The time is a guide: the turkey is done when a food thermometer reads 165 °F.

Can I cook a turkey that is still frozen?

Yes: the extension sources say it takes at least 50 percent longer than the times for a thawed turkey. Check 165 °F with a food thermometer.

Should I stuff the turkey?

The University of Georgia Extension advises cooking the stuffing separately in a casserole dish, for food safety and so the turkey cooks evenly. If you do stuff the bird, the center of the stuffing must also reach 165 °F on a food thermometer (University of Nebraska–Lincoln Extension). The roasting times in this planner are for unstuffed turkeys only: with a stuffed bird, go by the thermometer readings, not the clock.

How long should the turkey rest before carving?

About 20 minutes out of the oven, which is what the planner uses. If it reaches 165 °F early, cover it with foil while it waits.

How much turkey per person?

About 1 lb of whole turkey per person, 1½ lb for hearty eaters or leftovers, ½ lb of bone-in breast.
