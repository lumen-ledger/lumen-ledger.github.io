How it works

  1. Start = serving time minus cooking minutes minus resting (or holding) minutes. Out / done = serving time minus resting minutes. These are the same formulas as the "Menu" sheet of the Christmas Dinner Planner spreadsheet.
  2. Oven clash: two oven dishes clash when they need different temperatures and their cooking windows overlap. One home oven cannot do both, so the usual fixes are: cook one earlier and reheat it, move one to the stovetop or slow cooker, or bake it while the roast rests.
  3. Make-ahead dishes have no time and are listed separately, so they are not forgotten.
  4. The minutes are yours: cooking times depend on weight, shape, oven and recipe, so this page does not invent them. For a turkey, the turkey thaw and roast planner uses published roasting tables by weight.
  5. Nothing is sent anywhere. "Copy link to this plan" puts the menu after the # of the address, which browsers do not send to the server: open the link on another device to see the same plan.

Safe minimum internal temperatures

The timeline tells you when to start; a food thermometer tells you when it is done. From the U.S. CDC page "Preventing Food Poisoning" (dated November 24, 2025, read 2026-11-07):

FoodMinimum internal temperature
Whole cuts of beef, veal, lamb and pork, including fresh ham145 °F (63 °C), then let it rest 3 minutes
Ground meats such as beef and pork160 °F (71 °C)
All poultry, including ground chicken and turkey165 °F (74 °C)
Fish with fins145 °F (63 °C)
Leftovers and casseroles165 °F (74 °C)

Pre-cooked ham: follow the package label. Celsius values are rounded conversions. Source: cdc.gov/food-safety/prevention. General information, not professional food-safety advice.

FAQ

Why does my roast start so early? Because resting counts: a roast that cooks 150 minutes and rests 20 goes in 170 minutes before you eat.

Does it include preheating? No. Add your oven's preheat time before the first oven start, or include it in the cooking minutes of the first oven dish.

Can I also plan guests, shopping and budget? Yes, in the Christmas Dinner Planner (free spreadsheet, pay what you want; direct download christmas-dinner-planner.xlsx): the same timeline plus guest list, dietary needs and a shopping budget. For November, the same page exists for Thanksgiving dinner. For gifts, see the holiday budget calculator and the Secret Santa generator.
