USPS holiday shipping deadlines 2026

For delivery before Friday, December 25, 2026 within the contiguous US, USPS recommends sending by Dec. 17 (Ground Advantage, First-Class Mail), Dec. 18 (Priority Mail) or Dec. 19 (Priority Mail Express). Pick a destination and service below to see how many days you have left.

FAQ

What is the last day to ship with USPS for Christmas 2026?

Within the contiguous US, USPS recommends sending by Saturday, December 19, 2026 with Priority Mail Express. The other send-by dates are Thursday, December 17 for USPS Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail, and Friday, December 18 for Priority Mail.

Are the USPS holiday dates guaranteed?

No. USPS publishes them as recommended send-by dates and says delivery dates are estimates that vary with origin, destination and the time the item is accepted at the Post Office.

When should I mail packages to Alaska or Hawaii?

For First-Class Mail, Priority Mail and Priority Mail Express, USPS lists the same dates as the contiguous US (Dec. 17, 18 and 19). For Ground Advantage, USPS gives no single date: between the contiguous US and Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico or the territories it states a service expectation of 10 or more days, so check postcalc.usps.com for your ZIP codes.

What is the USPS international holiday deadline for 2026?

USPS lists Wednesday, December 9, 2026 for First-Class Package International and Priority Mail International, and Wednesday, December 16 for Priority Mail Express International, for all the regions it lists (Africa, Asia/Pacific Rim, Australia/New Zealand, Canada, Caribbean, Central and South America, Europe, Mexico, Middle East).

When should I mail to APO/FPO/DPO addresses?

The Ground Advantage date (November 4, 2026) has passed. USPS lists December 9 for First-Class Mail and Priority Mail, and December 16 for Priority Mail Express Military Service, which is not available to AE ZIP 093.

Does this page cover UPS and FedEx?

No. This page only reproduces the dates published by USPS. UPS and FedEx publish their own holiday schedules on ups.com and fedex.com.
