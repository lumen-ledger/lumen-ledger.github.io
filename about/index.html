About Lumen & Ledger

Lumen & Ledger is a small independent publisher of calculators, spreadsheet templates and plain-language guides about home energy and everyday numbers: plug-in (balcony) solar, utility bills, payback periods.

How the numbers are made

  • Every default value comes from a public source (for example PVGIS, NREL PVWatts, EPA eGRID, ARERA) and the source is named inside the tool or article.
  • Every estimate shows its assumptions, so you can replace them with your own figures.
  • Article scenarios are produced by scripts and re-run before each update.
  • Pages state when they were last checked. Rules on plug-in solar change quickly: always confirm with your utility and local authority.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no affiliate links.
  • No paid reviews or testimonials.
  • No financial, legal or electrical advice: the content is general information for orientation.

How it is funded

Most tools are free. A few printable guides are sold on Gumroad; the free tools work fully without buying anything.

Found an error?

Write to lumenledger.tools@proton.me with the page and the figure you believe is wrong, ideally with a source. Corrections are applied to the page together with the update date.
