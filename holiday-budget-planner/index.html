Holiday budget planner: one spreadsheet for gifts, food, travel

Set one number for the whole season, split it by category and by person, then log what you actually spend. Example: a $1,500 budget with $1,165 planned and $584.63 spent leaves $915.37 (39% used), and $436.24 of it is on credit cards.

"Everything except gifts" is food and hosting, travel, decorations, cards and postage, wrapping and shipping, events, donations. In the example, $895 is the sum of those rows on the Setup sheet. The calculator is plain arithmetic on your numbers: (total − other spending) × (1 − reserve) ÷ people.

If the holidays go on a credit card: what the interest costs

Runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

The default APR, 22.36%, is the Federal Reserve's average rate on credit card accounts assessed interest at commercial banks, Q3 2026 (preliminary; 21.19% across all accounts), from the G.19 Consumer Credit release of November 6, 2026. Your own rate is on your statement.

$1,000 at 22.36% APR, payingMonths to pay offInterest paid
$200 a month6$59.81
$100 a month12$117.05
$50 a month26$262.88

Method: each month interest = balance × APR ÷ 12 (rounded to the cent), then the payment is subtracted; no new purchases, fees or promotional rates. Card issuers usually compute interest on the average daily balance, so your statement can differ by a few dollars. If the payment does not cover the first month's interest, the balance never goes down and the calculator says so.

How to use it without overthinking

  1. Start from the total you can pay in cash or pay off in full on the next statement, not from the gift list.
  2. Fill the non-gift categories first: food, travel and shipping are harder to cut later than a gift.
  3. Give each person a budget on the Gifts sheet before shopping; the Summary compares the gift total with the Gifts category.
  4. Log purchases the day you make them, including shipping and wrapping, and pick the payment method so the credit-card total stays accurate.
  5. Next year, copy this year's actuals into "Last year actual" and the Summary shows the change.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I spend on each gift?

There is no right amount for everyone: it depends on the total you can pay off. With the calculator defaults ($1,500 total, $895 for everything except gifts, 12 people, 10% reserve) the gift pot is $605 and the budget is $45.37 per person. These are illustrative numbers, not national averages.

What goes into "everything except gifts"?

Food and hosting, travel, decorations, cards and postage, wrapping and shipping, events and outings, donations. Subtract them from the total before dividing: they are harder to cut later than a gift.

How much should I keep aside for surprises?

5-10% of the gift pot. In the example, 10% of $605 is $60.50: it covers a forgotten gift or a guest who joins at the last minute.

The amount per person is too low. What can I change?

Shorten the list (for example one group gift for friends or coworkers), lower a non-gift category or raise the total. Example: with the same defaults and 8 people instead of 12, the budget goes from $45.37 to $68.06 per person.

Why is the total a few cents below the gift pot?

Each budget per person is rounded down to the cent, so the total never goes over. With the defaults, 12 × $45.37 = $544.44 against $544.50 available after the reserve.

Does the spreadsheet work in Google Sheets?

Yes. It has no macros or add-ins and opens in Excel, LibreOffice Calc and Google Sheets (File → Import).

How do the return-by dates work?

On the Gifts sheet you type the store's return deadline for each purchase; the date turns orange 14 days ahead. The window is set by the store's policy; for UK online orders the gift return deadline calculator gives the legal 14-day cancellation date.

How much interest will I pay if I put the holidays on a credit card?

At 22.36% APR (Federal Reserve G.19 average for accounts assessed interest, Q3 2026), $1,000 paid off at $100 a month takes 12 months and costs $117.05 in interest; at $50 a month it takes 26 months and costs $262.88. Use the card interest calculator with your own APR and payment.

Does the calculator save or send my numbers?

No. The calculation runs in your browser; the page has no tracking, cookies or accounts, and the spreadsheet stays on your device.
